How it works

Operational hiring. No job-board theatre.

We don't dump a thousand resumes on your desk. We run a tight, accountable process from brief to placement.

  1. 01

    Brief

    You share the role, urgency and budget. We translate it into a precise candidate scorecard.

  2. 02

    Source & vet

    Our team sources, screens and runs structured interviews. Only candidates who score > 80% move forward.

  3. 03

    Shortlist

    You receive 3–5 prepared candidates with notes, references and salary alignment.

  4. 04

    Close

    We coordinate interviews, references and offer negotiation until your hire signs.
