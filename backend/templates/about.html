About

A recruitment operations company,
not another job board.

VOIDD Hire is the operational layer modern companies use to run hiring. We replace the chaos of inbound resumes with a tight, accountable process.

What we do

We work as an extension of your in-house team. From the first brief to the signed offer, we run sourcing, vetting, scheduling, references and offer negotiation — and report on it weekly.

What we don't do

We don't run a public job board. We don't forward unfiltered resumes. We don't take on roles we can't realistically close. Every brief is operationally scoped before we say yes.

Who we work with

Funded startups, growth-stage product companies and operationally-minded enterprises hiring across engineering, product, design and revenue functions.

Operating principles

How we work.

01 · Scorecard first

Every role starts with a written candidate scorecard. No scorecard, no search.

02 · Fewer, better

We send 3–5 vetted candidates per role. Quality of shortlist is the only metric that matters.

03 · Honest conversations

Salary clarity on both sides from day one. No surprises at offer stage.

04 · Operationally accountable

Weekly written updates. SLAs on response time. You always know where the search stands.

Terms & conditions

By submitting a profile or hiring brief, you agree that the information provided is accurate and may be used by VOIDD Hire to facilitate the recruitment process. We retain submissions for active matching for up to 24 months unless a deletion request is received. Placement fees are agreed in writing before any candidate is presented. Replacements are offered within the first 90 days of placement subject to the engagement letter.

Privacy policy

We collect contact details, resume content and the information you voluntarily submit through our forms. This data is stored in encrypted databases and shared only with hiring companies relevant to your stated preferences, or with talent matching your hiring brief. You may request access, export or deletion of your data at any time by writing to privacy@voiddhire.com.
